# Review decisions

## Choice

`review` takes 2–4 `decisions` of its own; without them it's approve/reject, unchanged. `value` is the labels given the first decision, so the answer union stays the same. Gaps under `none` fall back to the last decision, the most cautious one.

## Why

Per-item assignments were being crammed into multi-select plus Other (Logan, 2026-10-10, brief 11).
