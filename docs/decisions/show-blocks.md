# Show blocks

## Choice

A `show` question type with no answer: `content` (markdown) or `path` (with `lines`). A form needs at least one other question; `question` stays required as the lead line.

## Why

A large body had no home but the question text or one option's preview, and a form of only shows would be `notify`, which is parked (Logan, 2026-10-14, brief 16).
